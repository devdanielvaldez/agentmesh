# Code of conduct

AgentMesh is committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive community. Treat participants with
respect, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and make space for people with different
backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and sustained disruption are not
tolerated in project spaces or when representing the project. Maintainers may edit or remove
content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers' GitHub profiles. Reports will
be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Good-faith reports will not result
in retaliation.

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, community events, and other official
AgentMesh spaces.
